What Sentinel does when something goes wrong

Every protection runs on its own. You choose which ones are on with /protection toggle, and Sentinel posts everything it does in your log channel.

Someone's account gets hacked

  • Deletes QR codes that steal Discord logins
  • Quarantines anyone posting the same message in 3 channels at once
  • A honeypot channel catches spam bots and wipes their messages
  • Removes leaked tokens, passwords and Roblox cookies

Raiders show up

  • Removes mass joins and locks the door for new ones
  • Spots raid accounts sharing the same picture or name pattern
  • Disconnects voice chat raids
  • /panic locks the whole server in one command, and undoes it just as fast

Someone tries to nuke the server

  • Stops mass bans and kicks, and quarantines whoever did it
  • Kicks bots added by people you haven't trusted
  • Takes back Administrator handed out without permission
  • Rebuilds deleted channels, roles and emojis, with daily backups

You run a Roblox or ER:LC server

  • /roblox profile shows a full risk report for any Roblox account
  • Finds alts sharing one Roblox account
  • A verify button for new members, like Bloxlink
  • Pings your staff when a blacklisted player joins in-game

One blacklist for every server

When our team blacklists a scammer or raider, they're removed from every server running Sentinel, with proof saved. Anyone blacklisted can appeal, and you can always let a specific person into your own server.

Protected in three steps

No dashboard to learn. Everything happens inside Discord.

  1. Add Sentinel

    Invite it with the button above, then drag its role near the top of your role list so it can act on everyone below it.

  2. Run /setup

    Pick a log channel and turn on Blacklist protection. Every other setting is on the same panel.

  3. Test it with /drill

    Sentinel runs a fake raid, punishes nobody, and grades your setup from A to F with exactly what to fix.

Questions

Still stuck? Open a ticket in the support server.

Sentinel isn't doing anything in my server

An admin needs to run /setup and turn Blacklist protection on. Nothing runs until then.

I can't see Sentinel's commands

Press Ctrl + R to refresh Discord. You'll only see commands your role is allowed to use. Run /help to browse everything you can use.

Sentinel says it's missing permissions

Go to Server Settings → Roles and drag Sentinel's role near the top. It can only act on people whose highest role is below its own.

Sentinel stopped someone by mistake

The server owner can mark trusted people with /protection trust. Anyone quarantined gets every role back with /unquarantine.

How does someone get blacklisted?

Server staff report people with proof using /report. Only the Sentinel team can approve a blacklist, and every case keeps its evidence.

I was blacklisted. What can I do?

Run /appeal start. It works in DMs too. The team reviews it and messages you with the decision. If it's denied, you can appeal again after 14 days.

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

What Sentinel stores

  • Blacklist records: Discord user IDs, the reason, category, proof, and a history of each case.
  • Reports and appeals: who was reported, by which server, and what was written.
  • Moderation: warnings, timeouts and quarantines made through Sentinel, per server.
  • Roblox verification: your Roblox username and ID if you verify, and verify attempts.
  • Security logs: messages caught by protections (like scam links or honeypot posts) and which invite people joined with.
  • Server settings and backups: each server's setup, plus channel and role layouts and emojis for restoring after a nuke.
  • Support tickets: transcripts of tickets in our support server.

What Sentinel doesn't store

Normal chat messages are checked as they're sent and then forgotten. Sentinel doesn't keep message history, DMs, or voice.

How it's used

Only to protect servers: blocking blacklisted people, stopping attacks, and showing server staff what happened. We never sell data or use it for ads.

Who can see it

Server staff see their own server's logs. The Sentinel team sees blacklist cases, reports and appeals. Other servers only see what's needed, like whether someone is blacklisted.

Your choices

  • /mydata shows what Sentinel has stored about you.
  • /deletedata deletes your Roblox link right away and sends a deletion request for the rest. Blacklist records may be kept when they're needed to keep servers safe.

Payments are handled entirely by Discord. Sentinel never sees your card details.

Questions about your data? Open a ticket in the support server.
